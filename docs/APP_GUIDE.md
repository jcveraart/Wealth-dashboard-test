# App walkthrough

Start with [Installation](INSTALLATION.md) and the [screenshot tour](SCREENSHOTS.md).

The main navigation separates what you own from what you might explore. Investments contains owned holdings, performance, allocation/risk and savings plans. Explore contains opportunities, watchlists, company research and disclosure data. A cash account's purpose, liquidity and saved rate live in Savings & debt; fixed deposits and securities remain in Investments.

The home-style blue briefing gives context at the top right. Saved/demo observations are labelled; live provider calls require your personal setup. Ask about the page, choose a provider and response effort in chat, and keep the selected data context appropriate. Supported changes explicitly requested in chat can be saved with Undo; the dashboard never places broker orders.

Spending separates expenses, income and transfers. Click charts/categories to inspect payments. The small popup sorts by date or absolute amount, ascending or descending. Full transaction filters remain on Transactions. An invoice can explain several products inside one bank payment; confirm the matching instead of adding the invoice total as another expense.

Historical graphs use recorded values. Return metrics need sufficiently complete flows/valuations and disclose missing evidence. Savings-rate comparisons distinguish ongoing and introductory offers, amount tiers, access, source dates and legal-bank protection. Cash/debt scenarios use equal upfront money and monthly budgets and change no real records.

Settings contains appearance, provider setup, public-source coverage, local export, cloud copy and backups. Setup & help explains which choices are optional. Use the guide before enabling services or importing real records.
