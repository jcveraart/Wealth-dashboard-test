# Investment intelligence

Open Investments at `http://wealth.localhost/#holdings`. The existing Daily/History overview remains the starting point. Its navigation adds Portfolio Analysis, Opportunities, Superinvestors, Insiders, Watchlist, Research and Signals. Research also opens from a holding, an opportunity or search. Settings contains the same collapsed Data connections panel.

## What is integrated

Portfolio Analysis combines security exposure across accounts, classifies assets/sector/issuer domicile/listing currency, displays fund top-holding overlap and concentration, and calculates EUR adjusted-price volatility, drawdown, correlations, beta, Sharpe, Sortino, benchmark comparisons and contributions. Cost basis and unrealized gains use imported records. Realized gains require complete FIFO lots matching the remaining holding; disposed positions without retained lots are excluded. Missing data is visible rather than estimated.

Opportunities compares held, watched and researched companies, with filters for deteriorating earnings and diversification. It is not a whole-market stock screener. Each component exposes observed inputs, the rule, points and coverage. Seven weighted components are Quality (25%), Valuation (20%), Fundamental Change (15%), Portfolio Fit (15%), Smart Money (10%), Price Dislocation (10%) and Insiders (5%). Missing inputs are excluded; an overall score requires Quality, Valuation and Fundamental Change plus at least 40% weighted coverage. Scores are heuristics, not predictions; compare companies at similar coverage. Historical P/E comparison uses genuinely recorded snapshots, not fabricated backfills.

Superinvestors retains up to 20 recent 13F filings per manager at first ingestion, selects full restatements, preserves additions-only amendments, and shows full disclosed portfolios, historical quarters, changes, exits, weights and consensus. Add a manager by name and SEC CIK. `1067983` is Berkshire Hathaway. Values are USD dollars; filings before January 3, 2023 are converted from thousands. Securities are keyed by CUSIP, share/principal type and put/call, so options do not merge with shares. Changes in shares can reflect corporate actions rather than trades. 13F data describes quarter-end holdings and is delayed, normally by up to 45 days; shorts and many other assets are absent. Consensus may span different disclosed quarters. Company matching requires exact CUSIP; a held US/Canadian ISIN may supply it, or save a verified mapping in Research.

Insiders ingests issuer Form 4 records over 180 days, up to 60 filings per refresh. P/S non-derivative transactions without disclosed plans are prioritized. Awards, grants, options, conversions, tax withholding, gifts and disclosed automatic plans remain inspectable. No inference of discretion is made solely from missing plan disclosure. Tables expose insider, role, dates, code, classification, shares, price/value and ownership after transaction. Buying clusters, repeated purchases, monthly trends and purchases relative to verified market cap are computed in the backend. Exact matched amendments supersede originals; amended identities/quantities may require review. Coverage is explicitly limited to ingested filings.

Research combines company overview, financial statements/ratios, management and ownership, filings, insider activity, news, estimates, observed valuations, saved thesis notes and three configurable scenarios. Scenarios use trailing EPS grown by the user's annual assumption, exit P/E and constant cash dividends; they do not reinvest dividends or forecast actual returns. Nonpositive EPS produces unavailable values. Overview, Latest earnings, Bear case and Committee buttons open the existing chat; Committee requests seven separate perspectives and synthesis through the same model, not fictitious independent agents. Business moat, competitors, catalysts, risks, revenue segments, guidance changes and thesis conclusions require cited research or supported provider datasets. The model may interpret evidence; numerical portfolio metrics and scenarios must come from tools.

Watchlists retain notes and compare successive recorded observations. Signals cover concentration, institutional additions/new positions/exits/consensus, insider buys/sells/clusters, valuation thresholds, price dislocation, earnings changes, estimate revision changes, company filings and source-linked news to review. News alerts are review prompts, not assertions of materiality. First ingestion may surface already-public recent events. Rules are configurable globally or by ticker. Signals are deduplicated by source evidence and retained in a local notification outbox. Delivery is in-app; there is no email or push sender yet.

## Sources and credentials

**SEC EDGAR:** set `SEC_USER_AGENT="WealthDashboard contact@example.com"`, or save the identifying app name/contact email in Data connections. Saving explicitly enables transmission of that contact identity to SEC public-data endpoints. Local settings may use `sec_user_agent` or `contact_email` plus `sec_enabled: true`. No paid SEC credential is required. Requests reserve slots in the shared SQLite database (0.22 seconds apart across application threads/MCP processes), back off on transient failures, and pause for five minutes after a 403/429. SEC can still deny requests. Public filing XML, submissions and facts are cached. Annual US GAAP/IFRS facts are normalized by reporting duration/currency and later restatements, with concept-level filing evidence; unsupported concepts remain absent.

**Market data/OpenBB:** the existing Yahoo price loop remains. New historical analytics use replaceable adapters with split/dividend adjustment and dated FX conversion. Quotes are delayed. Market provider `auto` prefers installed OpenBB and falls back to Yahoo; forced `openbb` reports failure rather than silently changing source. OpenBB is optional and installed separately to preserve a Python 3.9 dashboard runtime:

```powershell
py -3.12 -m venv .openbb-env
.\.openbb-env\Scripts\python.exe -m pip install -r requirements-openbb.txt
```

An isolated Python 3.12 OpenBB environment is already installed on this laptop. The adapter detects `.openbb-env/Scripts/python.exe` (Unix: `bin/python`); `OPENBB_PYTHON` or local `openbb_python` can override it. Worker settings/logs stay in ignored `cache/openbb/`. The v4 compatibility profile contains official core, equity, economy, SEC and yfinance packages; v5 changed routers/providers, so replace `openbb_commands` and `openbb_price_command` before upgrading. `openbb_data_provider` defaults to `yfinance` (no additional key). Available statements, estimates, ownership, segmentation/geographic and macro routes are retained; unsupported datasets show unavailable. Paid provider credentials, if chosen, belong in the private OpenBB user settings, never in frontend code. Yahoo statements are the normalized baseline; supported SEC/OpenBB annual statements fill missing datasets without mixing currency or annual/quarter durations. OpenBB raw originals remain available for comparison.

**Quiver Quantitative:** optional. Obtain an API token for a subscription covering your desired datasets from [Quiver API setup](https://www.quiverquant.com/api-setup/). Set `QUIVER_API_KEY` or save a local `quiver_api_key` in Data connections. No key means no requests. Routes are verified against [Quiver's official schema](https://api.quiverquant.com/docs/schema.json): institutional `/beta/live/sec13f?ticker=...`, insiders `/beta/live/insiders?ticker=...`, and historical congress, government contracts, lobbying, all patents and executive compensation. Supported pagination is retained up to 10,000 rows per dataset/refresh, with truncation indicated. Dataset-level failures are isolated. Local `quiver_routes` may override supported live/historical paths. Quiver cannot be live-verified without your token; it is not used to fabricate score inputs.

**AI:** the existing Claude Code / Anthropic and ChatGPT-plan authentication and chat switches remain. Claude remains the default and logos are unchanged. OpenAI has no paid API-key fallback. The investment tool registry is shared by OpenAI function calls, Anthropic client tools and Claude Code's local MCP server. Provider usage/permissions still apply. Questions and retrieved financial context are sent to the chosen chat provider as part of answering; public web searches must exclude personal payment/account information. The local financial database and keys are excluded from Git.

## Data, calculations and architecture

`intelligence/data.py` providers → `sec.py` ingestion / `normalize.py` annual normalization → `store.py` SQLite → `analytics.py` deterministic metrics → `service.py` APIs/jobs → `web/intelligence.js` → `tools.py` / `investment_mcp.py`.

The app's existing JSON files, authentication, imports and Supabase sync remain. SQLite is additive because the existing app has no normalized historical investment database. Schema migration 1 creates observations, price series, filings, holdings, transactions, managers, watchlists, rules, signals, delivery outbox, jobs and settings. Initialization version 2 adds new default alert types without overriding saved rules. Database: `cache/intelligence.sqlite3`, excluded from Git and Supabase. It may contain private watchlist/thesis notes. Back it up locally with the financial files, using SQLite's backup API for a running application. Do not upload this database to a public demo.

Observations retain source, retrieval time, reporting period, currency, filing date, original URL and digest. Identical snapshots refresh retrieval time without duplicating payloads. Changed snapshots persist for comparison. Price series retain source, currency, date, adjustment flag and retrieval time; source replacement preserves observations rather than mixing duplicate days. New corporate actions trigger adjusted-history backfill. Fund look-through uses disclosed partial weights only; impossible totals above 100% are rejected. Unknown sector/geography/currency stays unknown. Issuer domicile and listing currency are proxies, not full revenue geography or economic currency risk. Style labels are simple descriptive proxies, not regression factors.

Risk uses constant weights of **covered current holdings**, normalized within the covered basket, with at least 60 common daily returns. Gaps over five days are excluded. It is not actual account performance, cash-flow-adjusted return or a historical holdings reconstruction. Coverage and periods are displayed. Sharpe/Sortino use the user-configured annual risk-free assumption (default 0%); no risk-free curve is invented. Benchmark prices are converted to EUR with dated FX; insufficient alignment leaves comparative returns unavailable. Optional fit risk analysis needs at least 90% portfolio coverage and 60 candidate-aligned returns. Return contributions show mean daily weighted basis points; risk contribution is weight × covariance with portfolio / portfolio variance. Cost basis data may be incomplete. None of these metrics is calculated by an LLM.

The scheduler runs only while this local app is running. It begins 15 seconds after startup, checks hourly eligibility each minute and defaults to refreshing every six hours. Company snapshots use a six-hour freshness window; historical price refresh starts seven days before the last stored date, except first load/corporate action backfill. Existing filing accessions are skipped; immutable historical submissions are cached. Refresh runs in a daemon thread with a single ingestion lock, progress cursor and persisted job status. Failed sources leave cached data usable and visible. Interrupted jobs are marked after restart. Offline mode disables ingestion and online price updates.

The loopback-only `/api/intelligence/*` read routes cover portfolio, opportunities, investors, insiders, watchlist, research, prices, alerts, summary and status. Write actions cover refresh, manager/watchlist/rule CRUD, seen signals, thesis/scenarios and connections; a stateless history-metrics action supplies calculations for existing History charts. Cross-origin and unexpected Host requests are blocked. Provider status returns flags only, never keys/contact details/tokens. Config updates whitelist fields. Private data changes still go through the app's existing actions and import validation.

## MCP tools

`investment_mcp.py` is a newline JSON-RPC stdio server. Chat launches it automatically, so no plugin installation is needed. For another local MCP client configure the application runtime as `command`, this file's absolute path as `args`, and the app directory as `cwd`:

```json
{"mcpServers":{"wealth":{"type":"stdio","command":"ABSOLUTE_PATH_TO_PYTHON","args":["ABSOLUTE_APP_PATH/investment_mcp.py"],"cwd":"ABSOLUTE_APP_PATH"}}}
```

Tools: get_portfolio, analyze_portfolio, get_position, get_company_fundamentals, get_company_valuation, get_price_history, get_superinvestor_holdings, get_13f_changes, get_insider_transactions, get_form4_activity, get_watchlist, get_opportunities, calculate_portfolio_fit, get_company_news, get_recent_filings, build_investment_thesis. Portfolio tools need no ticker; nullable account/asset-class filters default to all investments. Company tools need `symbol`; institutional tools accept nullable `cik` and `quarter`. Portfolio, opportunity, watchlist, price, holding, change and transaction detail tools support `limit`/`offset`, returning total counts and `next_offset`; follow pages to retrieve complete stored data. Analysis summaries show the largest 20 positions, 20 chart points and a 12-security correlation preview, while all totals and metrics use the full selected portfolio and aligned series. `get_position` supplies individual contributions/correlations; Research supplies complete scoring rules. Detailed original evidence remains in the app. No arbitrary shell/file/write tools are exposed; tools may cache public queries. Missing data produces explicit errors.

## Verification and limitations

Run `python -X utf8 -m unittest discover -s tests`. Regression coverage includes existing imports, receipts, OAuth/provider behavior and new units/annual periods, FIFO lots, exposure/risk/scenarios, missing data, invalid fund weights, amendments, optional keys, Quiver routes, shared SEC rate reservations, MCP and OpenAI continuations. Browser scripts are syntax-checked and UI flows tested against fictional holdings. Live tests verified OpenBB public quotes/profile, actual SEC 13F portfolios and Apple Form 4 / annual facts, plus Claude MCP and OpenAI structured portfolio tools using fictional holdings. Optional paid Quiver/provider datasets are implemented but cannot be live-tested without the appropriate credential. Segments, ownership, FX, complete insider histories and fund constituents depend on real source coverage; unavailable data is not replaced by placeholders or fake information.
